Hindi Recipes – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · App: Hindi Recipes (com.rasoininja.app) · Developer: Cricway Pvt Ltd

Hindi Recipes is an offline collection of Indian recipes in Hindi. All recipes are built into the app. We do not run a server for this app, we do not ask you to sign in, and we do not collect what you read.

What the app stores on your phone

Your settings (such as language) and anything you choose to save inside the app stay on the device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. We have no copy.

Favourites

Recipes you mark as favourite are saved only on your phone.

Food safety

Recipes are general home-cooking guidance. Check ingredients for allergies and cook food thoroughly.

Third-party services in the app

  • Google AdMob shows ads that keep the app free. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK) you are asked for consent first. See how Google uses information in ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Google Firebase Analytics records anonymous usage events (for example which screen was opened, app version, language, device model and an app-instance ID) so we can see which features people use and fix problems. It does not receive what you type or save in the app. See Firebase privacy.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share anything with anyone other than these services.

Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

We hold no account or personal data for this app, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. To remove advertising data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Analytics data held by Google is deleted automatically after 14 months.
