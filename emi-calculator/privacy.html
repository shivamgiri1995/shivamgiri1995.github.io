EMI Calculator – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · App: EMI Calculator (com.paisaninja.app) · Developer: Cricway Pvt Ltd

EMI Calculator is a set of offline calculators (EMI, SIP, GST, income tax, FD/RD and PPF). Everything you type is calculated on your phone. We do not run a server for this app, we do not ask you to sign in, and the amounts you enter are never sent to us.

What the app stores on your phone

Only your chosen language and a small counter that limits how often ads are shown. This stays on the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services in the app

  • Google AdMob shows ads that keep the app free. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK) you are asked for consent first. See how Google uses information in ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Google Firebase Analytics records anonymous usage events (for example which calculator was opened, app version, language, device model and an app-instance ID) so we can see which tools people use and fix problems. It does not receive the numbers you enter. See Firebase privacy.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share anything with anyone other than these services.

Children

The app is meant for adults planning their money. It is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

We hold no account or personal data for this app, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. To remove advertising data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Analytics data held by Google is deleted automatically after 14 months.

Calculations are estimates

Results are for planning only. Interest rates, tax rules and bank conventions change; please confirm with your bank, tax advisor or the official source before you decide.
